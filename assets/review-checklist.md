# Independent conversion review

Give the reviewer only the final artifact or accessible preview, buyer task, verified offer facts, and acceptance criteria. Do not give the author's preferred variant or diagnosis. Ask for evidence-linked findings; a reviewer should not invent a problem to fill a quota.

## Buyer task

State who is trying to do what, their acquisition context, device/language, and real commitment.

## Inspect

- Is every applicable baseline check from `SKILL.md` recorded with evidence as pass, fix, investigate, or not applicable, even when it falls outside the implementation shortlist?
- Were relevant families in the 360-tactic library scanned, selected entries matched to actual triggers, and exact changes tied to IDs? Does the work avoid duplicates and arbitrary additions?
- Were relevant persuasion patterns and templates considered, with selected PAT/TMP records applied to a concrete task? Does each filled template contain verified facts and usable output instead of unresolved placeholders or generic advice?
- Were appropriate local tools used when inputs were available, with assumptions and findings interpreted correctly? Are static/source checks distinguished from rendered behavior, verified payment and measured business effects?
- Do sales materials address the real buyer, economic decision and procurement path? Are proposed commercial changes and unsent communication drafts clearly identified within the user's existing authorization?
- Can the buyer understand the product, fit, value, evidence, total cost, and next step?
- Does each important claim have a real source? Are examples and proposals clearly identified?
- Does the CTA lead to the promised action and preserve the chosen plan and interval?
- Do displayed prices, limits, trial conditions, and renewal terms match actual behavior?
- Can the service deliver the advertised allowance under current capacity/spend limits? Are daily, account-level, and cumulative stops explained correctly?
- Does due-now wording match each purchase/account state, and was the affected provider payment/fulfillment path actually exercised or explicitly marked unverified?
- If pricing is in scope, is `assets/pricing-audit.md` complete, including the fresh-visitor default, both billing states for every tier, and a reasoned default decision?
- Does annual mode prominently show the monthly equivalent for comparable monthly/annual plans, with a legible adjacent annual charge and billing label? Is the comparison unit consistent across the switch?
- Are savings computed for each actual plan, upgrade value clear, and recommendation labels supported by fit or evidence?
- Does an annual-first default apply only to an eligible undecided visitor, while deliberate choices, valid intended links, account context, and experiment assignment remain respected?
- Were the switch in both directions, selection continuity, and actual checkout plan/interval checked rather than inferred from text appearing in the source?
- Can the journey complete with keyboard, mobile layout, and relevant error/recovery states?
- Are loading, pending, payment failure, success, and entitlement states truthful?
- Is important information available without misleading defaults, fake urgency, or obstructed exit?
- Does the metric capture the business goal with a defined denominator and window?
- Could missing data, sample-ratio mismatch, immaturity, interference, or repeated testing invalidate the interpretation?
- Do revenue, retention, refunds, and support outcomes contradict an apparent click/signup win?
- Are refund requests distinguished from issued refunds, with a meaningful denominator and comparable follow-up? Does the measurement miss buyers discouraged before registration?
- What could a screenshot or code-only review not establish?
