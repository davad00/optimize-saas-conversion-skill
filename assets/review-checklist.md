# Independent conversion review

Give the reviewer only the final artifact or accessible preview, buyer task, verified offer facts, and acceptance criteria. Do not give the author's preferred variant or diagnosis. Ask for evidence-linked findings; a reviewer should not invent a problem to fill a quota.

## Buyer task

State who is trying to do what, their acquisition context, device/language, and real commitment.

## Inspect

- Can the buyer understand the product, fit, value, evidence, total cost, and next step?
- Does each important claim have a real source? Are examples and proposals clearly identified?
- Does the CTA lead to the promised action and preserve the chosen plan and interval?
- Do displayed prices, limits, trial conditions, and renewal terms match actual behavior?
- Can the journey complete with keyboard, mobile layout, and relevant error/recovery states?
- Are loading, pending, payment failure, success, and entitlement states truthful?
- Is important information available without misleading defaults, fake urgency, or obstructed exit?
- Does the metric capture the business goal with a defined denominator and window?
- Could missing data, sample-ratio mismatch, immaturity, interference, or repeated testing invalidate the interpretation?
- Do revenue, retention, refunds, and support outcomes contradict an apparent click/signup win?
- What could a screenshot or code-only review not establish?

## Return

| | | | |

Finish with acceptance criteria met, criteria unmet, and checks unavailable. Distinguish functional/visual validation from measured business impact. If no independent reviewer was available, label the pass as self-review.
