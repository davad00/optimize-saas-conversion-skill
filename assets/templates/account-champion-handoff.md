# Account champion handoff

Template: `TMP-058` · Category: `lifecycle`

## Use when

Preserve useful adoption and billing continuity when the customer’s internal owner changes.

## Required inputs

- Current authorized account roles
- Active workflows and commitments
- Billing and integration ownership
- Supported transfer process
