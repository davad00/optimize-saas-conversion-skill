# Buyer-specific quote summary

Template: `TMP-037` · Category: `pricing`

## Use when

Summarize the complete configured purchase so buyers can compare and share it accurately.

## Required inputs

- Verified plan and quantity
- Currency, taxes, fees, and term
- Required add-ons or services
- Quote validity and provider/source

## Quote summary

**Prepared for:** [Pseudonymous account or approved customer reference].
**Configuration:** [Plan, seats, usage, region, and included services].
**Quote status:** [Estimate / authoritative quote; date and version].

| Component | Quantity and unit | Rate | Period | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Subscription] | [Quantity] | [Verified rate] | [Interval] | [Subtotal] |
| [Required setup/add-on] | [Quantity] | [Rate] | [One-time/recurring] | [Subtotal] |

**Due now:** [Actual or clearly estimated amount].
**Total committed cost:** [Defined term and assumptions].
**Renewal:** [Amount/rule, interval, and next applicable date].
**Taxes and variable usage:** [Included/excluded and calculation basis].
**Validity:** [Real expiry or statement that pricing may change before acceptance].
**Next step:** [Review/checkout/proposal route preserving configuration].

Keep a single authoritative version and show which facts remain estimates. Sharing this summary must not create a payment or alter an existing subscription.

## Completion check

Reconcile line items, interval, total commitment, and due-now amount against the source; confirm required costs and account-specific terms are included.
