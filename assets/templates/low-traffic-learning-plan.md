# Low-traffic learning plan

Template: `TMP-074` · Category: `measurement`

## Use when

Learn from a small customer base without pretending it supports a precise uplift estimate.

## Required inputs

- Available traffic and paid outcomes
- Observed defects and major objections
- Access to task sessions or customer evidence
- Decision horizon and implementation cost

**Uncertainty worth resolving:** [One consequential buyer or product question].
**Known defect to repair now:** [Observable issue with authorized reversible fix].
**Hypothesis:** [Specific change, affected buyer, and proposed mechanism].
**Meaningful downside:** [Refunds, support burden, failed tasks, or other practical risk].

| [Observed rollout] | [Outcome and guardrail] | [Eligible cohort] | [Practical review] | [Confounding] |

**Implementation batch:** [A coherent, small set of changes].
**Observation window:** [Enough time for the real buying process].
**Review date and owner:** [Concrete follow-up].
**Next larger test:** [Only if traffic and infrastructure become sufficient].

Report what customers understood or completed and what remains uncertain. Avoid declaring success from a few clicks, a short before/after period, or an arbitrary significance threshold.

## Completion check

Use evidence proportional to the decision, document assumptions, and separate functional improvement from an unmeasured claim of increased sales.
