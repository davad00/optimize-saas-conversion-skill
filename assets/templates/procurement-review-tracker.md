# Procurement review tracker

Template: `TMP-066` · Category: `sales`

## Use when

Remove avoidable buying delays by assigning requirements, evidence, and dependencies.

## Required inputs

- Buyer procurement requirements
- Approved current security/legal/product documents
- Actual reviewers and ownership
- Target decision process

## Review organization

**Independent tracks:** [Work that can proceed in parallel].
**True gating items:** [Decision that must precede another step].
**Consolidated questions:** [Single current list with answer source and owner].
**Document freshness:** [Version, scope, owner, and review date].
**Unmet requirement:** [Exact gap, possible supported alternative, and buyer decision needed].

Use approved materials and permitted sharing routes. A broad security claim cannot substitute for product-specific evidence, and the presence of a document does not mean the buyer accepted it. Keep legal interpretations with the appropriate responsible reviewer rather than inventing contractual assurances.

**Closeout evidence:** [Actual accepted requirement or unresolved blocker].

## Completion check

Verify document versions and access, retain unresolved requirements, and ensure every blocker has a responsible owner and a concrete next action.
