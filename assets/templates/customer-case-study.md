# Customer outcome case study

Template: `TMP-025` · Category: `landing`

## Use when

Explain how a real customer achieved a result and what another buyer can reasonably learn.

## Required inputs

- Approved customer account and quotations
- Baseline and measured outcome
- Implementation details and dependencies
- Publication permissions

## Story scaffold

**Title:** “[Customer type] used [verified capability] to [observed result].”
**Situation:** [Task, prior process, volume, constraints, and buying trigger].
**Decision:** [Alternatives considered and actual selection criteria].
**Implementation:** [Steps, customer effort, timeline, integrations, and service support].
**Result:** [Metric definition, before/after periods, denominator, and evidence].
**Limits:** [Other changes, uncertainty, what did not improve, and conditions required].
**Customer voice:** [Approved quotation with relevant context].
**Transfer question:** “Does your situation share [important conditions]?”
**Next step:** [Demo or offer matching the case].

| Result claim | Source | Measurement caveat | Approved wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Specific result] | [Record] | [Attribution limitation] | [Exact sentence] |

A before/after story can describe a customer's experience without establishing that the product caused the entire difference. Keep that distinction in the published wording.

## Completion check

Verify every numerical claim and permission, preserve contextual limitations, and ensure the linked plan actually supports the demonstrated workflow.
