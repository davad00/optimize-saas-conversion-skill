# Evidence-backed proof card

Template: `TMP-024` · Category: `landing`

## Use when

Turn an approved customer or product result into a credible proof block.

## Required inputs

- Permissioned quotation or measured result
- Identity and relationship disclosure
- Baseline and result scope
- Source, date, and intended placement

## Proof card

**Claim or quotation:** “[Approved wording preserving the original meaning].”
**Who and context:** [Customer/role/organization if approved; relevant buying situation].
**Starting point:** [Baseline, task, or previous method].
**Observed result:** [Outcome, period, denominator, and measurement method].
**Conditions:** [Implementation effort, sample size, package, or material limitation].
**Relationship:** [Any material sponsorship, incentive, or other relationship].
**Source link:** [Full approved story or underlying method].

## Placement decision

- Buyer doubt answered: [Specific question].
- Adjacent page claim: [Exact claim the card supports].
- Evidence freshness: [Date and product version].
- Alternative if permission is unavailable: [Anonymous aggregate or product demonstration that can be supported].

Use only fields applicable to the evidence. Do not turn an individual experience into a typical-result guarantee or rewrite a quote into a stronger claim.

## Completion check

Check permission, attribution, wording, measurement scope, and nearby implications; the card must remain accurate when viewed without the rest of the case study.
