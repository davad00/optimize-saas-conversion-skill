# Trial decision summary

Template: `TMP-052` · Category: `lifecycle`

## Use when

Help an evaluator judge paid fit using actual results and remaining requirements.

## Required inputs

- Verified trial terms and end state
- Actual completed customer tasks
- Known blockers and required paid capabilities
- Authorized communication scope

## In-product or draft message

**Your evaluation:** [Dates and actual trial conditions].
**Completed work:** [Verified useful outcomes, with accessible artifacts].
**Still unresolved:** [User-relevant unanswered questions or blocked tasks].
**Paid continuation:** [Capabilities and allowance needed for the declared workflow].
**Payment transition:** [Actual charge timing, amount, interval, and card requirement].
**If you do not continue:** [Accurate access and data consequences].
**Next action:** [Choose plan / finish evaluation / get specific help].

| Evaluation criterion | Evidence achieved | Remaining check | Owner or available route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Criterion] | [Actual artifact/result] | [Question] | [Action] |

Use the customer's actual work rather than a generic countdown. Do not imply a trial exists when the product has none, or invent success for an inactive account. Message content is a draft; sending requires explicit authorization.

## Completion check

Verify trial timing and transition behavior, ensure claimed outcomes occurred, and confirm the suggested paid plan supports the evaluator’s real use case.

Related tactics: `UPG-016`, `OFR-001`, `POS-014`.
