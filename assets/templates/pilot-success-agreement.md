# Pilot success agreement

Template: `TMP-064` · Category: `sales`

## Use when

Define a bounded evaluation that can produce an interpretable adoption decision.

## Required inputs

- Buyer task and representative workload
- Product and service capacity
- Pilot terms and any approved credit
- Evaluation owners and data handling

## Pilot definition

**Status:** [Proposed or already authorized terms].
**Scope:** [Users, tasks, integrations, data, duration, and excluded work].
**Supplier deliverables:** [Access, support, implementation, or reports].
**Buyer contributions:** [Inputs, people, decisions, and effort].
**Commercial terms:** [Exact price, payment, credit, expiry, and transition; mark each term proposed or already authorized and identify any decision still required].

| Success criterion | Baseline | Evaluation method | Acceptance evidence | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Meaningful outcome] | [Current state] | [Defined task] | [Observable threshold] | [Role] |

**Failure or interruption:** [Supported recovery and effect on timing].
**End-of-pilot decision:** [Adopt, extend under defined conditions, or stop].
**Data and access at end:** [Retention, export, and removal].
**Uncertainty:** [What this pilot cannot establish about larger-scale operation].

Do not choose success criteria after seeing the result. Keep a paid pilot distinct from an automatically renewing subscription unless that transition is explicitly part of the authorized terms.

## Completion check

Verify deliverability, acceptance criteria, ownership, and end conditions; make any proposed commercial change concrete before requesting a missing decision.
