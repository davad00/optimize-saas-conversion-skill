# Decision-focused FAQ block

Template: `TMP-027` · Category: `landing`

## Use when

Answer remaining purchase questions directly at the relevant page location.

## Required inputs

- Observed buyer objections
- Current product and policy sources
- Related support material
- Placement and next-step context

## FAQ entry scaffold

**Question:** “[Buyer wording for one concrete uncertainty].”
**Direct answer:** [One accurate sentence].
**Conditions:** [Plan, region, input, timing, or other limits that change the answer].
**Example:** [Illustrative calculation or workflow, clearly labeled when hypothetical].
**Evidence/detail:** [Current documentation or source].
**Next action:** [Return to choice / check fit / request relevant help].

| Question | Decision blocked | Fact owner | Best page location | Publication-ready? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Avoid burying required payment terms inside collapsed FAQ content. Keep actual charge, renewal, and essential limitations visible in the main offer. Group only related questions, use accessible disclosure controls, and return buyers to their prior plan or task after opening supporting detail.

## Completion check

Check each answer against current policies, test keyboard disclosure behavior, and ensure essential terms remain visible without opening the FAQ.
