# Product demo storyboard

Template: `TMP-026` · Category: `landing`

## Use when

Show one useful task and its result without making the evaluator sit through an unrelated tour.

## Required inputs

- Priority buyer task
- Working product and safe sample input
- Actual plan and prerequisites
- Known wait and failure states

## Demo sequence

| Beat | Screen or action | Narration | Buyer question answered | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Result preview | [Finished useful output] | [What it is] | What will I get? | [Real output] |
| Input | [Required material] | [Prerequisite] | What must I provide? | [Supported input] |
| Decision | [Meaningful control] | [Why it matters] | How much control do I have? | [Actual behavior] |
| Processing | [True state] | [Realistic expectation] | How long does it take? | [Measured/qualified] |
| Review | [Inspect/edit output] | [Limits and next step] | Can I use this? | [Observed result] |

**Recoverable failure:** [One relevant failure and resolution].
**Plan label:** [Package used; extra dependencies].
**Continuation:** [Carry result or selected task into the accurate next action].

Mark sample data and edited highlights clearly. Offer access to the fuller workflow when omission would change perceived effort or capability.

## Completion check

Run the demonstrated task end to end, preserve realistic waiting and control, and avoid showing unsupported features as available.
