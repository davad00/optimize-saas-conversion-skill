# Hero copy and action block

Template: `TMP-022` · Category: `landing`

## Use when

Make the first screen explain the customer, outcome, mechanism, and next step.

## Required inputs

- Priority buyer and task
- Verified product capability
- Real proof asset
- Accurate CTA destination and commitment

## Fillable hero

**Eyebrow, if useful:** [Audience or workflow category].
**Headline:** “[Observable outcome] for [specific customer or task].”
**Support:** “[Product] [concrete action/mechanism], so you can [supported consequence]. [Important prerequisite].”
**Primary action:** [Immediate action the button actually performs].
**Commitment line:** [Card/trial/price/setup fact relevant to that action].
**Product visual:** [Real screen or labeled example showing the promised result].
**Proof caption:** [Approved evidence supporting the principal claim].

## Alternative emphasis

| Version | Customer obstacle addressed | Changed explanation | Fact held constant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [A] | [Incomprehension] | [Mechanism-led] | [Real offer] |
| [B] | [Unclear payoff] | [Result-led] | [Same offer] |

Choose a coherent emphasis using available buyer evidence. Keep qualifications visible near quantified claims. Inspect the narrow layout so headline, price context, and primary action remain understandable without decorative elements pushing them apart.

## Completion check

A buyer should be able to explain what it does, whether it fits, and what happens next; verify every claim and action destination.
