# Sales-to-implementation handoff

Template: `TMP-068` · Category: `sales`

## Use when

Preserve the exact customer promise and success criteria after a purchase.

## Required inputs

- Accepted proposal and actual contract
- Verified account and entitlement
- Evaluation findings and buyer roles
- Implementation scope and open commitments

## Handoff record

**Customer objective:** [Agreed task and outcome].
**Purchased scope:** [Actual package, quantities, services, term, and account].
**Promises made:** [Exact commitment, source, owner, and due date].
**Exclusions and limits:** [What was explicitly outside scope].
**Success criteria:** [Evidence agreed during evaluation].
**Customer roles:** [Operator, sponsor, billing, technical owner].

| Implementation milestone | Required input | Owner | Dependency | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [First useful setup] | [Data/access] | [Role] | [Prerequisite] | [Usable result] |

**Open issue:** [What remains unresolved and who owns it].
**First customer action:** [Specific useful step, not a generic tour].
**Review point:** [When actual value and expectation match will be checked].

Do not silently convert a sales conversation into an unsupported delivery obligation. If accepted documents conflict, identify the discrepancy and route it to the responsible owner before making a new promise.

## Completion check

Reconcile the handoff with accepted terms, ensure first-value ownership is clear, and verify the customer receives the capabilities and support actually purchased.
