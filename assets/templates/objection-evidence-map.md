# Objection and evidence map

Template: `TMP-009` · Category: `research`

## Use when

Place the right answer and supporting proof where a specific buying doubt appears.

## Required inputs

- Customer questions or observed hesitation
- Verified product documentation
- Approved customer proof
- Journey surfaces and buyer roles

## Objection register

| Buyer question in their words | Moment and role | Real answer | Evidence and date | Placement | Missing fact owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [“Will this work with our files?”] | [Before signup; operator] | [Supported scope] | [Compatibility test] | [Beside upload example] | [Owner] |

## Reusable response block

**Question:** [One specific doubt].
**Direct answer:** [Accurate answer, including a limiting condition].
**Show:** [Demonstration, documentation, case, or comparison proving that answer].
**Next step:** [Check compatibility / view sample / ask a relevant question].

Rank objections by observed frequency, consequence, reach, and strength of the available answer. Keep unsupported claims out of publishable copy. Where the product cannot meet the need, explain the boundary and offer a useful route rather than reframing the limitation away.

**Placement hypothesis:** [Why this answer belongs beside this decision].
**Learning check:** [Comprehension or qualified progression measure].

## Completion check

Each answer must have a source and placement; ensure the evidence supports the exact claim and does not imply a broader capability.
