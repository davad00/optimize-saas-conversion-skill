# Promotion and renewal timeline

Template: `TMP-034` · Category: `pricing`

## Use when

Make introductory discounts and later recurring charges understandable before purchase.

## Required inputs

- Approved offer terms
- Eligibility and actual expiry
- Provider billing schedule
- Renewal, cancellation, and tax rules
