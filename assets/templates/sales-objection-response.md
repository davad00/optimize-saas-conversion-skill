# Sales objection response card

Template: `TMP-067` · Category: `sales`

## Use when

Help a seller respond to a specific live concern without guessing the buyer’s reason.

## Required inputs

- Buyer’s exact objection
- Verified product and commercial facts
- Relevant evidence
- Permitted concessions and authority

## Conversation card

**Buyer statement:** “[Actual wording].”
**Clarifying question:** “[Neutral question distinguishing likely causes].”
**Acknowledgment:** “[Accurate restatement of the concern].”
**Answer:** [Direct fact with its conditions and limitations].
**Evidence:** [Relevant demonstration, calculation, documentation, or case].
**Choice:** [Supported next step, alternative package, or honest no-fit conclusion].

| Interpretation | Evidence needed | Suitable response | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [No budget] | [Actual process/constraint] | [Real purchase route] | [Invented financing] |
| [Unclear value] | [Task and criteria] | [Relevant evidence] | [Unsupported ROI] |
| [Implementation risk] | [Required effort] | [Concrete plan] | [Unapproved guarantee] |

**Commercial exception:** [Only what is already authorized; otherwise prepare a specific proposal].
**Outcome to record:** [Concern resolved, new question, or poor fit].

Use the card as a conversation aid, not a script for arguing past a legitimate limitation.

## Completion check

Confirm the concern before responding, cite evidence that answers it, and do not invent concessions or treat silence as agreement.
