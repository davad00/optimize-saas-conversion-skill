# Usage-limit notice and next-step block

Template: `TMP-053` · Category: `lifecycle`

## Use when

Explain an approaching allowance limit before it unexpectedly interrupts useful work.

## Required inputs

- Current verified usage and allowance
- Reset timing and timezone
- Supported overage or upgrade options
- Account role and dismissal behavior

## Notice copy

**Current usage:** “[Used quantity] of [allowance] [defined units].”
**Timing:** “[Allowance resets at actual time]” or [accurate nonresetting/cumulative rule].
**Effect at the limit:** [Pause, blocked new work, overage, or other actual behavior].
**Relevant option:** [Upgrade, authorized temporary capacity, wait for reset, or current-plan alternative].
**Financial consequence:** [Actual amount and term if an option costs money].

| Threshold | Reason for notification | Placement | Dismissal and repeat rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Evidence-based threshold] | [Enough time to act] | [Relevant workflow] | [Respect explicit dismissal] |

Distinguish daily, monthly, per-account, and cumulative service limits. Do not describe a resettable allowance as available when another cumulative cap prevents delivery. Use verified workload to suggest capacity and make its assumptions visible.

## Completion check

Reconcile the notice with actual enforcement and reset behavior, test account roles and stale data, and verify each offered route preserves unfinished work.
