# Expired campaign recovery page

Template: `TMP-021` · Category: `acquisition`

## Use when

Give visitors a useful and truthful route when an old promotion or campaign ends.

## Required inputs

- Original campaign promise and expiry
- Current verified offers
- Known incoming links
- Product continuity and support options

## Recovery page copy

**Heading:** “[Named offer] ended on [actual date and timezone if relevant].”
**Explanation:** [Brief factual reason or campaign context].
**Current path:** “You can still [supported product outcome] with [current offer or normal plan].”
**Primary action:** [View current offer / compare plans / continue task].
**Contextual help:** [Useful route for an existing purchase or eligibility question].

| Incoming route | Original expectation | Current destination | Preserved context | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Link] | [Promise] | [Route] | [Task or valid account state] | [Observed result] |

Do not silently replace an expired special price with a higher checkout total. Do not restart a countdown or imply the old promotion remains available. Preserve existing customer entitlements and distinguish campaign expiry from loss of purchased access.

**Measurement:** [Recovery to qualified evaluation and paid outcomes; billing-confusion contacts].

## Completion check

Exercise old URLs and return paths, verify the expired terms are not purchasable accidentally, and ensure the current offer is clearly identified.

Related tactics: `INT-013`, `CTA-018`.
