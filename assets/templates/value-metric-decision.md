# Value-metric decision worksheet

Template: `TMP-031` · Category: `pricing`

## Use when

Evaluate which billable unit buyers can understand, predict, and connect to value.

## Required inputs

- Actual product usage and cost drivers
- Customer outcome and forecast ability
- Existing billing capabilities
- Support and budget objections
