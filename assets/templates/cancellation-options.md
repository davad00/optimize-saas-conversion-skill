# Cancellation, pause, and downgrade options

Template: `TMP-056` · Category: `lifecycle`

## Use when

Offer relevant alternatives while keeping the requested exit clear and operable.

## Required inputs

- Actual cancellation and refund policies
- Available pause/downgrade behavior
- Data retention and export rules
- Current subscription state

## Exit flow

**Requested action:** [Cancel renewal or end service, accurately distinguished].
**Immediate consequence:** [Access, bill, refund status, and data].
**Optional relevant alternative:** [Pause or lower plan if it addresses the stated reason].
**Continue cancellation:** [Clear action available without unrelated tasks].

| Choice | Cost and timing | Remaining access | Data consequence | Confirmation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cancel | [Actual terms] | [End date] | [Export/retention] | [Verified state] |
| Available alternative | [Actual terms] | [Capability] | [Consequence] | [Explicit choice] |

**Optional reason question:** [Skippable, concise, with no blocked exit].
**Confirmation copy:** “[Renewal canceled at time]. [Access remains until date]. [Refund status if applicable].”

Do not treat canceling renewal as an automatic refund. Keep a pause or downgrade optional and avoid preselecting an alternative after the user chose cancellation.

## Completion check

Complete the actual cancellation path, verify billing state and confirmation, and ensure export remains usable under the documented policy.
