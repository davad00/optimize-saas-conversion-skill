# Campaign-to-page message map

Template: `TMP-015` · Category: `acquisition`

## Use when

Keep the acquisition promise, offer, and landing-page next step consistent.

## Required inputs

- Campaign creative and audience
- Destination URLs and tracking conventions
- Verified offer eligibility
- Landing-page content and CTA destinations

## Promise continuity

| Entry creative | Promise and qualification | Destination section | Visible matching evidence | CTA and carried context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Ad/email/listing] | [Exact claim and audience] | [Route/anchor] | [Headline/demo/terms] | [Action + valid parameters] |

## Landing variant scaffold

**Headline:** [Restate the supported promised outcome].
**Support:** [How it works and prerequisites].
**Proof:** [Evidence matching the acquisition claim].
**Offer:** [Verified plan, price, eligibility, expiry if real].
**Action:** [Accurate next step preserving the intended offer].

Check mobile entry, direct visits, shared links, authentication, expired offers, and returning users. Sanitize tracking parameters and avoid putting private data in URLs. A campaign context must not override an explicit buyer selection or a valid existing subscription state.

**Failure fallback:** [Honest route when a capability or offer is unavailable].
**Measure:** [Qualified paid outcome by original entry cohort, plus misleading-promise complaints].

## Completion check

Open each destination from its real entry format and verify the promise survives through the actual selected offer and checkout.
