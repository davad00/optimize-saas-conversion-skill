# Champion approval memo

Template: `TMP-061` · Category: `sales`

## Use when

Give an internal supporter a concise document they can use in their own approval process.

## Required inputs

- Buyer-defined problem and evaluation results
- Verified offer and implementation plan
- Actual approval roles
- Open risks and alternatives

## Forwardable internal memo

**Decision requested:** Approve [specific purchase or evaluation] for [team/task].
**Problem:** [Current process and evidenced consequence].
**Proposed solution:** [Product/package and supported workflow].
**Why this option:** [Decision criteria and actual alternatives considered].
**Evidence:** [Evaluation results, references, and unresolved questions].
**Cost and commitment:** [Due now, term, recurring cost, setup, and variable charges].
**Expected benefit:** [Buyer assumptions and measurement plan; no guaranteed ROI].
**Implementation:** [Owner, effort, dependencies, and first success milestone].
**Risks and controls:** [Material limitations and realistic mitigations].
**Decision timing:** [Buyer’s actual deadline and reason].

| Approver question | Answer | Supporting link | Remaining owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Question] | [Verified answer] | [Evidence] | [Owner] |

Write in language the champion can edit. Keep the memo accurate when forwarded without a salesperson’s explanation. Preparing it does not authorize sending it to stakeholders.

## Completion check

Ensure costs, benefits, risks, alternatives, and ownership are complete enough for internal review; separate verified results from forecast assumptions.
