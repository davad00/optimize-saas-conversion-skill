# Support-ticket obstacle coding

Template: `TMP-014` · Category: `research`

## Use when

Turn recurring support questions into specific conversion and onboarding repairs.

## Required inputs

- Redacted support tickets with dates
- Issue-resolution outcomes
- Affected plan and journey stage
- Current help and product behavior

## Coding sheet

| Ticket ID | Customer task | Journey stage | Observed obstacle | Root cause verified? | Resolution | Reusable fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Pseudonymous reference] | [Task] | [Evaluation/signup/use] | [Question or error] | [Evidence/unknown] | [Actual outcome] | [UI/copy/product/help] |

Use separate codes for missing explanation, missing capability, technical failure, incorrect expectation, and account-specific exceptions. Preserve multiple causes when needed rather than forcing each case into one convenient label.

## Pattern summary

**High-consequence pattern:** [Repeated obstacle and affected users].
**Evidence boundary:** [Ticket sample, dates, selection bias].
**Proposed repair:** [Exact surface and change].
**Help placement:** [Where users need the answer before opening a ticket].
**Escalation:** [Cases requiring product or billing investigation].

Compare support contact rate with eligible or active users when a valid denominator exists. Falling ticket volume alone may reflect lower usage or harder contact routes.

## Completion check

Redact personal data, retain unresolved cases, and distinguish support-topic frequency from prevalence among all prospective customers.
