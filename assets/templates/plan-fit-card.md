# Plan-fit card

Template: `TMP-029` · Category: `pricing`

## Use when

Help buyers recognize the right plan through use case, capacity, and meaningful limits.

## Required inputs

- Current verified catalog
- Priority use case per tier
- Exact prices and billing intervals
- Real feature and capacity differences

## Card scaffold

**Plan:** [Stable plan name].
**Best suited to:** [Recognizable working need, not an unsupported popularity claim].
**Primary price:** [Comparable unit appropriate to the billing state].
**Actual charge:** [Amount, interval, tax convention, and commitment].
**Included outcome:** [Useful task enabled by this package].
**Capacity:** [Exact allowance and reset behavior].
**Key differences:** [Two or three decisive capabilities].
**Boundary:** [Important limitation or prerequisite].
**Action:** [Choose plan, with selected interval preserved].

| Buyer workload | Expected fit | Assumption | When to choose another plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Concrete workload] | [Supported capacity] | [Explicit units] | [Verified threshold or need] |

Keep monthly and annual cards aligned for comparison. If translating requests into tasks, state the assumed requests per task and acknowledge variability. The presentation cannot expand the delivered allowance or override service capacity limits.

## Completion check

Verify source prices, arithmetic, allowance resets, actual entitlements, and all supported billing states; complete the mandatory pricing audit separately.
