# Offer comprehension session

Template: `TMP-013` · Category: `research`

## Use when

Discover whether buyers understand the product, price, commitment, and next step.

## Required inputs

- Current page or prototype
- Representative buyer task
- Verified offer answer key
- Recruitment and note-taking approach

## Session plan

Show [page/state] under [device/context]. Ask participants to describe the offer in their own words before revealing the answer key.

1. What would this product help you do?
2. Who does the offer appear to suit?
3. What would you pay today, and what happens at renewal?
4. What is included, and what happens if usage exceeds the allowance?
5. What do you expect after selecting this button?
6. What information would you still need before deciding?
