# Current sales proposal

Template: `TMP-065` · Category: `sales`

## Use when

Present one authoritative offer with scope, cost, dependencies, and acceptance steps.

## Required inputs

- Qualified buyer requirements
- Verified current quote and package
- Implementation commitments
- Authorized terms and proposal version

Keep one authoritative current proposal. Do not imply acceptance, signature, or commercial approval from a draft, meeting, or unconfirmed email. Prepare the proposal fully within the authorized scope.

## Completion check

Reconcile proposal scope and numbers against the current quote and contract, resolve conflicting versions, and verify the stated acceptance route.
