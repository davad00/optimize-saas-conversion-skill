# Customer language lexicon

Template: `TMP-010` · Category: `research`

## Use when

Replace internal jargon with accurate language buyers use to describe their work.

## Required inputs

- Permissioned transcripts or support excerpts
- Product terminology and domain definitions
- Target audience and language
- Existing headlines and field labels

## Copy transformations

**Current:** “[Existing sentence].”
**Proposed:** “[Specific customer task] with [verified mechanism], so [observable result].”
**Evidence:** [Source/date and segment].
**Necessary technical term:** [Term], meaning [short explanation at first use].

Retain technical terminology when precision matters to the audience. Translate the task rather than replacing every unfamiliar word with a vague benefit. Keep billing units, plan names, and permissions consistent across pages, forms, invoices, and help content.

## Editorial acceptance

- Can a reader describe the actual action after reading the sentence?
- Does the preferred term mean the same thing to operators and purchasers?
- Which locales need a task-based translation rather than a literal idiom?

**Owner and review trigger:** [Product change, customer evidence, or localization update].

## Completion check

Check replacement terms against product behavior and preserve meaning; quotes establish observed language, not permission to publish customer identities.
