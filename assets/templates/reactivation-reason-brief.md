# Reason-based reactivation brief

Template: `TMP-057` · Category: `lifecycle`

## Use when

Prepare a relevant return path when the specific reason for leaving has changed.

## Required inputs

- Documented departure reason
- Verified product or offer change
- Account retention and restore policy
- Explicit outreach authorization if messaging

## Relevance decision

**Previous obstacle:** [Customer-stated reason and date].
**What changed:** [Shipped capability, resolved issue, or available offer].
**Evidence:** [Current documentation or observed behavior].
**Who still should not return:** [Remaining mismatch].

## Draft invitation or in-product return screen

“You previously needed [documented requirement]. [Specific supported change] is now available. You can review [evidence/demo] and, if useful, [accurate next step]. [Current terms or remaining limitation].”

**Restored context:** [Work that still exists and can be recovered].
**Unavailable history:** [Deleted or expired material, disclosed accurately].
**Open problem suppression:** [Do not promote through an unresolved support or billing dispute].
**Sending status:** [Draft only; verify explicit authorization and applicable preferences before contact].

Measure renewed useful use and retained paid outcomes, not reply rate alone. Do not assume a product change resolves every departure reason.

## Completion check

Verify the original reason, real change, current permissions, and restored state; ensure claims about retained data and pricing are accurate.
