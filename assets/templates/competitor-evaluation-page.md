# Factual competitor evaluation page

Template: `TMP-017` · Category: `acquisition`

## Use when

Help buyers compare real alternatives on criteria relevant to their work.

## Required inputs

- Dated primary sources for both products
- Comparable workload and package
- Buyer criteria
- Verified pricing and capability limitations

## Comparison page scaffold

**Audience:** [Who is making this choice and why].
**Summary:** “[Our product] fits [need]; [alternative] may suit [different need].”

| Decision criterion | Our supported behavior | Alternative's documented behavior | Source/date | Consequence for this buyer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Criterion] | [Exact scope] | [Exact scope] | [Links] | [Practical trade-off] |

**Comparable cost example:** [Same seats, usage, term, required add-ons, and currency].
**Migration reality:** [Import scope, effort, gaps, ownership].
**Try the decisive task:** [Relevant demo or evaluation route].

Separate absence of documentation from proven absence of capability. Mark custom pricing and unknown terms clearly. Avoid selective plan comparisons that make one product appear cheaper by omitting required costs.

**Maintenance trigger:** [Release or price change; owner/date].
**Primary measure:** [Qualified evaluations reaching paid use].

## Completion check

Every comparative claim must be sourced and current enough for the claim; retain the situations in which the alternative is a better fit.
