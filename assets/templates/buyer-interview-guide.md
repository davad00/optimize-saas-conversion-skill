# Buyer interview guide

Template: `TMP-007` · Category: `research`

## Use when

Investigate the circumstances behind a real buying or abandoning decision.

## Required inputs

- Interview objective and segment
- Recent purchase, evaluation, or abandonment example
- Consent and recording preferences
- Known product and offer facts

## Interview script

**Opening:** “We are trying to understand how you handled [task]. There are no right answers; please describe what actually happened.”

1. “Take me back to the last time you needed [outcome]. What triggered the search?”
2. “What were you using before? What made it insufficient then?”
3. “Which alternatives did you seriously consider? How did you compare them?”
4. “What nearly stopped you? What information changed your mind?”
5. “Who else had to agree, and what did they need to know?”
6. “What happened immediately after the decision? Where did expectations differ?”

Ask for concrete examples before proposing explanations. Do not introduce the desired answer or pitch an improvement during discovery.
