# Switching-cost comparison

Template: `TMP-036` · Category: `pricing`

## Use when

Help buyers compare adoption effort and ongoing cost against their actual current workflow.

## Required inputs

- Buyer current tools and contracts
- Verified replacement capabilities
- Migration effort and dependencies
- Current offer and any authorized transition credit

Use a matched workload and explicit assumptions; verify each replaced function and keep proposed credits separate from approved invoices.
