# Partner referral destination

Template: `TMP-020` · Category: `acquisition`

## Use when

Explain a referring relationship and continue a partner-specific buying context.

## Required inputs

- Authorized partner relationship and claims
- Referral offer and eligibility
- Shared customer use case
- Tracking and contact permissions
