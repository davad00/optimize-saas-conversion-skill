# Purchase confirmation and first-use handoff

Template: `TMP-044` · Category: `purchase`

## Use when

Confirm a verified payment and give the buyer a direct route to useful access.

## Required inputs

- Confirmed payment and entitlement state
- Receipt and renewal facts
- Receiving account
- Relevant first-value task and support route

## Confirmation content

**Status:** “[Payment/subscription state actually confirmed].”
**Purchased:** [Package, quantity, interval, and receiving workspace].
**Paid:** [Actual amount and currency; receipt route].
**Next billing:** [Verified date or schedule and renewal terms].
**Access:** [Entitlement active / provisioning status with honest expectation].
**First action:** [Specific useful task appropriate to the purchased package].
**Help:** [Safe support reference and recovery path].

| Exceptional state | Message | Recovery | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Payment confirmed, access pending | [Accurate state] | [Status/reconciliation] | [System/team] |
| Browser return missing | [Recover from account] | [Verified server outcome] | [System] |
| Wrong workspace suspected | [Clarify receiving account] | [Supported assistance] | [Team] |

Avoid premature celebration when the payment remains unresolved. Do not require a successful browser redirect for access if the server has already verified the purchase.

## Completion check

Reconcile receipt, account, amount, entitlement, and next charge; verify first-use and recovery links lead to the correct authorized workspace.
