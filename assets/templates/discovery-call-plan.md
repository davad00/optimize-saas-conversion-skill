# Sales discovery call plan

Template: `TMP-059` · Category: `sales`

## Use when

Qualify a current buying opportunity and identify the next useful evaluation step.

## Required inputs

- Prospect-stated goal and meeting context
- Verified product boundaries
- Known participants and buying stage
- Authorized conversation scope

## Meeting opening

“By the end of this conversation, we should understand [task], determine whether [product] can help, and agree on [useful next step] if there is a fit.”

## Discussion guide

- Current task and trigger: [What changed and why now?]
- Consequence: [What happens if the current process continues?]
- Requirements: [Must-haves, constraints, workload, integrations].
- Buying roles: [User, evaluator, approver, budget owner, procurement].
- Budget process: [How spending is approved; actual constraints].
- Decision timing: [Buyer’s real event and dependencies].
- Success evidence: [What must be demonstrated to proceed].

| Finding | Buyer evidence | Product fit | Open question | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Need] | [Statement/example] | [Supported/unsupported/unknown] | [Question] | [Action] |

**Close:** [Recap actual needs, acknowledge gaps, and propose a specific next step].

Do not manufacture urgency or treat a meeting as a qualified opportunity without the required evidence.

## Completion check

Record qualification evidence, unresolved gaps, and buyer-agreed next steps; route poor-fit prospects honestly rather than promising roadmap capabilities.
