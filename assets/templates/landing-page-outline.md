# Landing-page decision outline

Template: `TMP-023` · Category: `landing`

## Use when

Arrange page sections around the questions a buyer must answer before proceeding.

## Required inputs

- Primary acquisition promise
- Buyer questions and objections
- Verified offer and proof
- Available product visuals and CTA

## Page sequence

| Section | Buyer question | Exact content to supply | Proof or interaction | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First view | Is this for my task? | [Outcome, audience, mechanism] | [Product/result] | [Main CTA] |
| Workflow | How would I use it? | [Few meaningful steps] | [Input/output] | [Inspect or try] |
| Fit | Does it meet my requirements? | [Capabilities and boundaries] | [Specific evidence] | [Compare] |
| Offer | What do I receive and pay? | [Verified terms] | [Plan comparison] | [Select] |
| Remaining doubt | What could block success? | [Important objection answers] | [Documentation/proof] | [Proceed/help] |

**Section to remove:** [Redundant or irrelevant content and reason].
**Mobile order adjustment:** [Decision-critical material placed earlier].
**Repeated CTA rule:** [Same immediate action, consistent context].

Use the sequence as a starting structure, then reorder by the actual buyer's task. Do not force every section onto a narrow campaign page.

## Completion check

Each section must answer a distinct buying question; verify terms, CTA continuity, mobile order, and absence of unsupported claims.
