# Buyer-specific sales demo runbook

Template: `TMP-060` · Category: `sales`

## Use when

Demonstrate the prospect’s decisive workflow and capture evaluation evidence.

## Required inputs

- Discovery findings and agreed criteria
- Working product and safe buyer-relevant sample
- Attendee roles
- Actual package and limitations

## Demo contract

**Buyer task:** [One workflow from discovery].
**Decisive criteria:** [What attendees need to verify].
**Demonstration package:** [Actual plan, configuration, and dependencies].
**Success boundary:** [What this demonstration can and cannot establish].

| Sequence | Buyer question | Live action or evidence | Expected result | Fallback if unavailable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Step] | [Criterion] | [Actual workflow] | [Supported output] | [Honest alternative] |

**Operator view:** [Usability and control].
**Approver view:** [Business consequence and implementation effort].
**Technical view:** [Relevant limits, permissions, and integration facts].

## Close and record

- Criteria demonstrated: [Evidence].
- Criteria unresolved: [Owner and follow-up].
- Buyer corrections: [What differed from their real task].
- Next evaluation action: [Agreed useful step].

Avoid narrating unrelated features or presenting a prerecorded result as a live successful run. A fallback should disclose its limitations.

## Completion check

Use evidence tied to agreed criteria, label sample or prerecorded material, and record genuine unresolved requirements before proposing purchase.
