# Commercial offer proposal

Template: `TMP-035` · Category: `pricing`

## Use when

Prepare a concrete pilot, guarantee, bonus, credit, or installment offer for an authorized decision.

## Required inputs

- Observed purchasing obstacle
- Current terms and constraints
- Cost and operational capacity
- Existing authorization and required decision owner

## Completion check

Confirm the promise can be delivered and accounted for; never treat a completed proposal as authorization to change live commercial terms.
