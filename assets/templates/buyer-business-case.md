# Buyer-owned business case

Template: `TMP-062` · Category: `sales`

## Use when

Build a reviewable purchase case using the buyer’s economics, objectives, and uncertainty.

## Required inputs

- Buyer baseline and accounting conventions
- Verified offer and implementation costs
- Benefit assumptions and evidence
- Decision horizon and required return criteria

## Decision frame

**Objective:** [Customer-defined outcome].
**Alternatives:** [Status quo and realistic options].
**Horizon:** [Comparable period].
**Accounting basis:** [Cash, capacity, or other clearly separated measure].

**Calculation:** [Transparent formulas, units, and timing].
**Nonfinancial criteria:** [Control, quality, continuity, or capability].
**Break-even dependency:** [Assumption most capable of changing the decision].
**Validation plan:** [Pilot or measured task that checks that assumption].
**Recommendation:** [Fit under stated conditions and remaining uncertainty].

Do not count time released, payroll reduction, and revenue from the same hours as additive benefits without justification. Let the buyer edit every assumed value.

## Completion check

Reconcile time periods, currency, one-time costs, and double counting; clearly distinguish a model from realized savings or causal proof.
