# 75 reusable sales and conversion templates

Six existing templates plus 69 additional task-specific templates. Open a linked file, replace placeholders with verified facts, and use the relevant scaffold. Select only the outputs needed for the task.

See the [template selector](references/template-library.md) and [Python tools](references/python-tools.md).
